## How booking works

To book a spot to get your nails done, just tell Poppy:

- Your name
- The day and time you'd like to come in
- The service you want (optional — we can help you choose)
- A preferred technician (optional)
- A phone number (optional, so we can reach you about changes)

Poppy will save the appointment and confirm the details back to you. The name and a
time are enough to reserve; everything else is optional.

## Changes and cancellations

Need to change or cancel? Just ask Poppy or call the salon. Please give us at least
a few hours' notice so we can offer the slot to someone else.

## Deposits

Standard services do not require a deposit. Large groups or long custom nail-art
sessions may need one — Poppy will let you know if that applies.
