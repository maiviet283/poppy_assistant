## Meet our nail artists

You can request a specific technician when you book, and Poppy will note it on your
appointment.

- Mai — specializes in gel and intricate nail art.
- Linh — specializes in spa pedicures and nail care.
- Ngoc — specializes in press-ons and quick sets.
- Trang — specializes in classic mani/pedi and natural nails.

## Requesting someone

If your favorite artist is fully booked at your preferred time, we will let you know
and suggest the closest opening with them, or another artist who is a great fit.
