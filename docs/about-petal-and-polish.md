## About us

Petal & Polish is a nail salon and at-home nail-care shop. We do salon services
in-store — manicures, gel, pedicures, press-ons and nail art — and we also sell
long-wear polish, gel systems, press-ons and nail-care products you can use at home.

## Where we are

We are located at 123 Blossom Lane, Suite 4. Walk-ins are welcome when we have
openings, but booking ahead guarantees your spot and your preferred nail artist.

## How to reach us

You can chat with Poppy right here, call us through the assistant, or email
hello@petalandpolish.example. For appointments, the fastest way is to ask Poppy to
book you a spot.

## Payment

We accept all major cards and contactless payments in the salon. Online product
orders are paid at checkout on the website.
