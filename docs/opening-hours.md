## Opening hours

Our salon is open:

- Monday to Friday: 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM
- Sunday: 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM

The last appointment each day starts one hour before closing, so the artist has
time to finish your set.

## Holidays

We are closed on major public holidays. If a holiday falls on your requested day,
Poppy will suggest the next available time.

## Best times to visit

Weekday mornings and early afternoons are usually the quietest. Saturdays and late
afternoons are our busiest times, so book ahead if that suits you best.
