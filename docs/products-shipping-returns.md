## Shopping our products

Besides salon services, we sell at-home nail products: long-wear polish, gel
systems, press-ons, and nail-care treatments and tools. You can browse and buy them
on the shop pages of the website.

## Shipping

We offer free standard shipping on orders over $55. Orders usually ship within 1–2
business days, and standard delivery takes about 3–5 business days.

## Returns

Unopened products can be returned within 30 days for a refund. For hygiene reasons,
opened polish or used tools can't be returned unless they arrived damaged or faulty —
in that case we'll replace them right away.

## Product help

Not sure which shade or system to pick? Tell Poppy the look you want (bold, neutral,
seasonal) or the service you're trying to recreate at home, and she'll suggest options.
