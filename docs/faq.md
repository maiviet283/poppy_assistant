---
title: Frequently asked questions
---

## Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, when we have openings. Booking ahead through Poppy guarantees your spot and your
preferred artist, especially on weekends.

## How long do gel manicures last?

Gel manicures typically last about two weeks without chipping, depending on your nails
and daily activities.

## Can I bring my own design or inspiration photo?

Absolutely. Share the idea when you book (or bring a photo in), and we'll match it as
closely as we can. Detailed nail art is priced per nail.

## Do you remove gel or press-ons from another salon?

Yes. Our Soak-Off / Removal service gently removes gel or press-ons, whether we
applied them or not.

## Is there parking?

There is street parking on Blossom Lane and a public lot one block away.
